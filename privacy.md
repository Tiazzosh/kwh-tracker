# Privacy Policy for kWh Tracker

Last updated: 23 August 2026

kWh Tracker respects your privacy. This policy explains how information
is handled when you use the application.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores charging-session information entered by the user,
including charging dates, energy amounts, electricity prices, costs,
and charging sources.

This information is used only to provide the app's charging history,
statistics, and cost-calculation features.

## iCloud synchronization

If iCloud is available, charging information may be stored in the
user's private iCloud database so it can synchronize between the
user's Apple devices.

The developer does not have access to the contents of the user's
private iCloud database. Apple's handling of iCloud information is
governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Optional external synchronization

The app can connect to a synchronization service configured by the
user. Connection details are provided by the user, and the access key
is stored securely in the device Keychain.

Information exchanged with that service is subject to the privacy
practices of the service selected or operated by the user. The
developer does not operate or receive data from user-configured
services.

## Tracking and advertising

The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or
cross-application tracking.

The developer does not sell user information.

## Data retention and deletion

Charging information remains on the device or in the user's private
iCloud database until the user deletes it.

Users can delete individual records from within the app. Users can
also remove locally stored information by deleting the app and manage
synchronized information through their iCloud account.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed specifically at children and does not
knowingly collect children's personal information.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data
practices change. The latest version will remain available on this
page.
