# kWh Tracker Support

Thank you for using kWh Tracker.

## Contact

For support, feedback, or feature requests, contact:

me@mattiafalchi.com

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description
of the problem. Do not send passwords, access keys, or other
sensitive information.

## Common questions

### Why is iCloud synchronization unavailable?

Confirm that you are signed into iCloud on your iPhone and that
iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings.

### Is vehicle synchronization required?

No. Charging sessions can be added and managed manually. External
vehicle synchronization is optional and requires a compatible
service configured by the user.

### How do I delete my information?

Individual charging sessions can be deleted inside the app. To remove
all locally stored app information, delete the app from the device.
Data synchronized through iCloud can also be managed through the
user's iCloud account.

or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.
